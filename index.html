Generative Manufacturing (GenM)

Generative Manufacturing (GenM) is the manufacturing paradigm in which AI compiles human intent into fulfillable physical outcomes, so the demand side speaks in intent and verified engineering paths make good on it.

For about two hundred years, the people who make things and the people who use them have been kept apart. That separation made mass production possible. It also brought waste, sameness, and powerlessness: building before you know who wants what, everyone picking from the same short menu, and most people unable to get anything made without speaking the producer's language.

Generative Manufacturing is my definition of the shift that starts to undo that separation.
